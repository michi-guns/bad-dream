# Horror studies contract

Protocol: `chapter-horror-studies` 1.0.0. Status: active.

Purpose: learn how specific player experiences produce horror. Inputs are real
play sessions, observable events and accurately attributed sources. The boundary
is analysis: study findings do not automatically approve a Bad Dream feature.

Record event and reaction first, then interpretations and competing explanations.
Compare evidence across studies and propose a small testable experiment. Outputs
are linked study/evidence notes and justified proposal or task candidates. Quality
means clear separation between observation and inference, context (including
spoilers where needed), uncertainty and a useful next question. A negative or
inconclusive result is valid evidence.

Use [the optional study template](../templates/horror-study.md). Apply findings to
concept notes through explicit links. Technology trials can test hypotheses;
they cannot establish emotional effects without player evidence. A task candidate
needs a question, bounded play/review session and a useful completion criterion.
No requirement exists to finish a 20–25-game pool.
