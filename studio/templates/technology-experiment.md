# Technology experiment starting point

Use note metadata appropriate to an `experiment`. Predictions are not measurements.

## Question and baseline

State the bounded question, comparison and criteria for a useful answer.

## Method and measurements

Describe environment, reproducible steps, limits and what will be measured.

## Result

Record actual observations, failed runs and limitations. Leave pending until run.

## Decision and next action

Explain what evidence supports, what remains unknown and whether a proposal is
needed. An accepted investigation does not approve its hypothetical feature.
