# Horror study starting point

Identify the game/source, session context and spoiler scope. Do not imply a play
session occurred until it did. Use note metadata appropriate to a `study`.

## Event

What observably happened? Include enough context to interpret it.

## Reaction

What did the player report or do? Distinguish self-report from observation.

## Interpretation

What might explain the reaction? Include competing explanations and uncertainty.

## Testable experiment

What small test could distinguish explanations? Define outcome and limits.
Link evidence and related concepts; an experiment suggestion is not approval.
