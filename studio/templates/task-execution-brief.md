# Task execution brief

Use this inside a supported Backlog.md description/plan section. Keep it brief;
link the applicable workflow and add the task-specific method. Do not add custom
frontmatter or maintain a second copy of task status.

## Working method

- **Work type:** guided brainstorming / research / implementation / playtest / review.
- **Working surface(s):** where each stage happens, required capabilities and handoffs.
- **Human role:** input, participation and decisions.
- **Agent role:** preparation, facilitation, work and recording.
- **Preparation:** relevant notes, accepted scope, workflow/chapter and tools.
- **Procedure:** concrete steps; for brainstorming, the discussion questions.
- **Outputs and destinations:** durable records and any review/acceptance gate.
- **Completion verification:** evidence tied to acceptance criteria.

## Opening orientation

Explain what this is about, why it matters now, established versus open matters,
how we will work, what the human contributes and what result we will preserve.
Use concise bullets, bold or a table. Ask guided questions one at a time.

The agent supplies context; the human does not need to remember the repository.
See [task lifecycle](../workflows/task-lifecycle.md) for authority and legacy-task handling.

## Visibility and handoff

- **Applicable workflow and stable stage values:** link the shared procedure;
  establish task-level stages once only when no applicable workflow exists.
- **Current stage:** simple label at useful orientation points, not every reply.
- **Progress evidence:** meaningful known total or open scope; actual outcomes,
  explanation of reopened/changed counts; no implication of acceptance/integration.
- **Every response:** brief bullet Recap and next-action Recommendation(s).
- **Surface transition:** announce destination/reason and provide the complete
  receiving prompt from [handoff](handoff.md), preserving existing authority.
