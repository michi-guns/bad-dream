# Decision starting point

Record status and scope, accepting operator, date, exact accepted proposal
revision/fingerprint and actual provenance. Without these, keep it a draft.

Capture context, evidence/assumptions, alternatives, decision, practical rationale,
tradeoffs, consequences and reconsideration triggers. Link affected notes/tasks.
When superseded, retain the old reasoning and link its replacement, reason, date
and acceptance provenance. Add a reciprocal link from the replacement.
