# Project orientation

Bad Dream is the working title for a horror PC game. The operator is a full-stack
developer new to game development, working in free time with a friend. The studio
is solo-led and AI-assisted, with occasional human specialists, and may develop
over four to five years with long breaks. These are established context, not a
fixed delivery promise.

The authorised setup establishes a durable Git-backed operating workflow.
Creative ideas remain exploratory. Applies to: [dream concept](dream-concept.md).
No engine, visual style, AI model/provider or production scope has been accepted.

Practical advice to investigate includes small prototypes, human playtests,
reconnecting branches that preserve consequences, and learning alongside research.
A prospective 20–25-game library is a candidate pool, not an obligation. Selecting
and justifying the first few studies is future work.

The immediate milestone is a recoverable workspace and review of the creative
brief. Exit criteria: durable navigation, verified task tooling, honest setup
limitations, and a next action visible in [current state](../../studio/state/current.md).
