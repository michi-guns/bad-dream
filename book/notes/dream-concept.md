# Exploratory dream concept

Candidate creative directions include nested dream worlds, false awakenings,
persistent objects, dream contamination, branching consequences and a potentially
adaptive companion. None is an approved production mechanic.

One hypothesis is that carrying a recognisable object between apparently separate
dreams could make the player question the boundary between them. This is a proposed
explanation, with no playtest evidence yet. Alternatives include fixed narrative
clues, environmental continuity or a simpler single-world structure. Complexity
and predictability may weaken the effect rather than improve it.

Open questions: what decisions does the player make, what emotions are intended,
which dream rules are coherent, and what scope fits available hours? Engine,
visual style, runtime-AI value, latency, costs, offline behaviour and model choice
are also unresolved. Reconsider this concept if a small human playtest fails to
produce the intended uncertainty, or if the continuity rules confuse rather than
intrigue players.
